# 14: Works on a phone

**What to build:** The whole flow on a small screen — setup, watching a match, and the result — per the mobile column in `Wirefirms.png`.

The key layout decision: on a phone the default view is the board plus the shared speech log, with the thoughts panels behind a tap. The conversation is what makes a match legible at a glance; raw reasoning is the thing you go looking for (SPEC §9.2).
